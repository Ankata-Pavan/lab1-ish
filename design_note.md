## 1. PEAS description

## 2. Tool design decisions

Why did you design each tool's interface the way you did? What alternatives did
you consider (e.g. calculator safety, lookup matching, file sandboxing)?

What system-prompt choices did you make and why? What happened when you tried
different prompts?

Describe at least **two** specific failures you observed (and logged). For each:
what went wrong, why, and what a better design would look like.

## 5. Reflection
