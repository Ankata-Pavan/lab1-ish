CSE 410/510 - Introduction to Agentic Engineering
University at Buffalo - Fall 2026

Instructors: Dr. Joaquin Carbonara and Dr. David Doermann
Meeting time: Tuesdays and Thursdays, 11:00 AM - 12:20 PM
Office hours: by appointment

This course introduces the engineering principles behind tool-using AI
agents: the perception-action loop, the ReAct pattern, tool registries,
function calling, and the failure modes that make agents brittle. Students
build a series of increasingly capable agents, starting from a minimal
tool-using loop and progressing to multi-agent systems.

Grading: 6 labs (60%), midterm project (15%), final project (25%).
